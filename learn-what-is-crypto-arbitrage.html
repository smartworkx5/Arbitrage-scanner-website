What is crypto arbitrage?

A plain-language introduction to price gaps, the four main strategies and why most gaps are small.

Updated 30 September 2026

Arbitrage means taking advantage of a price difference for the same thing in different places, or of prices that are inconsistent with each other. In crypto, the same coin can trade at slightly different prices on different exchanges, in different pools and in different markets such as spot and perpetual futures.

The main types

  • Cross-exchange arbitrage: buy low on one exchange, sell high on another.
  • CEX vs DEX arbitrage: exploit differences between order-book and pool prices.
  • Triangular arbitrage: loop through three pairs on one exchange.
  • Funding-rate cash-and-carry: hold spot, short the perpetual, aim to collect funding.

Why gaps are usually small

Many participants watch the same gaps, and automated systems close them quickly. What remains is often small, so costs matter more than the headline percentage.
