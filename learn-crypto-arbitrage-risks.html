The real risks of crypto arbitrage

Arbitrage sounds safe, but execution, counterparty and market risks are real. Here is what to understand first.

Updated 30 September 2026

Arbitrage has a reputation for being low risk. In practice, the risk moves from "will the price go up?" to "can I execute the whole trade at the prices I expect?"

Execution risk

If one leg fills and the other does not, you are left holding a directional position you did not plan.

Counterparty and platform risk

Funds held on an exchange are exposed to that exchange's failures, freezes and withdrawal limits.

Smart contract and on-chain risk

DEX trades depend on contracts and on network conditions such as congestion and gas spikes.

Market risk in carry trades

A funding-rate carry position can face margin pressure and changing funding rates even when hedged.

How to reduce surprises

  • Study strategies in paper trading first.
  • Start small if you ever go live.
  • Never use money you cannot afford to lose.
