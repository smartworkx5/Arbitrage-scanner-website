About the paper trading bot

Why net numbers matter. A 0.6% price gap sounds good until you subtract two trading fees and a transfer cost. The calculator above shows how quickly that happens. The scanner is built around that reality.

Common questions

Is Crypto Arbitrage Matrix free to open?

The bot described on this site is a paper trading bot: it uses simulated funds. See the paper trading page.

No. Fees, slippage, transfer delays and fast-moving prices can turn a promising spread into a loss. Read the risk disclosure.
