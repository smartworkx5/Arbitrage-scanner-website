Paper trading bot

Test arbitrage ideas with simulated funds.

What it is

The automated paper trading bot acts on scanner opportunities using simulated money. It gives you a safe place to see how a strategy behaves over time, with fees and timing included.

Why use it

  • Learn how much of a headline spread survives costs
  • Compare strategies before committing capital
  • Build confidence in your rules without risking real funds
  • Spot mistakes while they are still free

A note on realism

Paper trading is useful, but it is not identical to live trading. Simulated orders do not face real queue positions, real slippage or exchange outages. Good paper results do not guarantee good live results.
