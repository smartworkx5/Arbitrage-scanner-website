Frequently asked questions

Short answers about the scanner and about arbitrage.

What is Crypto Arbitrage Matrix?

It is a real-time cryptocurrency arbitrage terminal. It scans cross-exchange spot prices across 16 exchanges, compares CEX and DEX pools, finds triangular arbitrage loops that need no transfers between exchanges, scans funding rates for delta-neutral cash-and-carry setups, and includes an automated paper trading bot.

What is crypto arbitrage?

Crypto arbitrage means profiting from price differences for the same asset, or from inconsistent prices between related markets. You buy where it is cheaper and sell where it is more expensive, or exploit gaps between pairs or between spot and futures. See our guide: what is crypto arbitrage.

Which strategies does the scanner cover?

Four: cross-exchange spot arbitrage, CEX vs DEX pool arbitrage, triangular arbitrage loops, and funding-rate cash-and-carry (delta-neutral) yield.

How many exchanges does it scan?

The spot scanner covers 16 exchanges. It also compares centralized exchange prices with decentralized pool prices.

What is triangular arbitrage?

Triangular arbitrage trades through three pairs on a single exchange, for example A to B, B to C, then C back to A, looking for a loop that ends with more of the starting asset. Because everything happens on one exchange, no funds are transferred between venues.

What is a cash-and-carry funding rate trade?

You hold an asset on the spot market and take an equal short position in its perpetual futures contract. Price moves offset each other, so the position is delta-neutral, and the aim is to collect funding payments when the rate is positive. Funding rates can change or turn negative, so the yield is not guaranteed.

Does the app place real trades for me?

The automated bot is a paper trading bot that uses simulated funds. It is meant for testing and learning.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Launch it in your browser. Supported browsers let you add it to your home screen or desktop.

Can I lose money with arbitrage?

Yes. Fees, slippage, delayed transfers, failed orders and fast price changes can all turn an apparent profit into a loss. Nothing on this site is investment advice.
